# 📚 Librory

> A cozy, judgment-free reading tracker for the mobile era. Designed for lapsed readers. No backend. No shame. Just books.

---

## The Problem: Reading Guilt & Competitive Tracking

In the modern digital age, reading has somehow transformed from a peaceful escape into a competitive sport. Modern reading trackers often feel like spreadsheets or social networks:
* **The Shame of Broken Streaks:** Miss a single day, and your months-long streak resets to zero, making you feel like a failure.
* **Clinical, Corporate Design:** Clean, sterile, white interfaces that feel more like project management tools than a cozy reading nook.
* **Competitive Metrics:** Constant comparison with others, pushing you to read faster or read *more*, rather than enjoying the book in your hands.
* **Loss of the Physical Connection:** No space for the tactile feel of reading—the condition of your physical copy, your handwritten margin notes, or the simple joy of placing a bookmark.

When life gets busy and we pause reading, these apps make us feel too guilty to return. 

---

## The Solution: Librory

**Librory** is a warm, offline-first mobile companion designed to help you rebuild your relationship with reading—entirely on your own terms. 

We believe that reading should be a cozy ritual, not a daily chore. Whether you read one book at a time or transition between five, whether you finish a chapter a day or a page a week, Librory is designed to gently welcome you back, never to shame you.

### Our Core Philosophy

* **Cozy Over Clinical:** Soft, warm color palettes, gentle transitions, and organic designs inspired by candlelight and old paper.
* **Judgment-Free Design:** Your reading streak never dies. If you take a break, your progress flame simply glows a little dimmer, waiting to be fanned back to life when you are ready.
* **Respect for Physical Books:** Space to record the physical edition, notes on who gave it to you, and your own cozy handwritten-style marginalia.
* **Optimized for Re-entry:** The app’s primary goal is helping you pick a book back up after a busy week, not punishing you for the gap.

---

## How It Works

Librory is built around a simple, comforting rhythm:

1. **Effortlessly Capture Your Shelf:** Simply pan your phone camera over your physical bookshelf. Librory instantly identifies and digitizes your collection in batches—no tedious, manual barcode scanning required.
2. **Start a Cozy Session:** Whenever you sit down to read, start a session. If the app closes or your battery dies, your progress is automatically saved.
3. **Tend Your Ember:** Instead of a punishing numerical streak, you tend a glowing fire. If you miss a few days, the ember dims. Reading just a few pages fans the flame back to life.
4. **Discover Your Style:** Over time, Librory learns how you read and reveals your **Reader DNA Archetype** (like the *Night Owl*, the *Juggler*, or the *Deep Diver*)—helping you understand your natural rhythms without judgment.

## Cozy & Advanced Features

### 👁️ Computer Vision Book-Spine Reconstructor
* **Real-Time AR Bookshelf Sync:** Skip the friction of turning books over to find barcodes. Point your camera at a shelf of books, and the app isolates individual spines, straightens perspective skews, and reads the titles in real-time.
* **Intelligent Glow Overlay:** Watch your physical shelf come alive in AR. Books already in your collection glow with a warm amber boundary, while newly detected titles light up with a soft haptic pulse as they are seamlessly added to your digital shelf.
* **On-Device Fuzzy Resolution:** Utilizing a localized token-matching framework, the app handles highly stylized fonts, vertical text layouts, and noisy real-world lighting to accurately resolve titles entirely offline.

### 🕯️ Warm & Adaptive Design
* **Time-of-Day Lighting:** The app's interface shifts naturally with the sun—from morning sky-blue to afternoon gold, evening amber, and cozy night-time candlelight.
* **Living Bookshelf:** See your books beautifully aligned, with glowing spines representing how recently you've held them.
* **Completion Ceremonies:** Finish a book and celebrate with a soft, satisfying custom animation.

### 📝 Marginalia & Memories
* **Handwritten Notes:** Jot down thoughts, quotes, or feelings in a beautiful script font that feels like writing in the margins.
* **Reading Journeys:** Look back at a timeline of when you read, what bookmarks you placed, and the thoughts you recorded along the way.

### 🧠 Librory Insight Engine
* **Physical-to-Digital OCR:** Snap a photo of any physical page to highlight and digitize your favorite quotes instantly. 
* **Semantic Threads:** View a cozy, physics-based interactive web of floating books connected by similar themes (e.g., *grief*, *hope*, *power*). This "Constellation of Thought" dynamically adapts to your **Reading Persona**, reshaping the graph to reflect your unique reading style and semantic journey.
* **The Remembrance Engine:** Receive gentle thematic callbacks on your Re-Entry Card, connecting what you are reading now with thoughts you recorded months ago in other books.

### 🎯 Mindful Motivation
* **Re-entry Assistance:** If you've been away for more than three days, Librory presents a gentle "welcome back" card with a tiny, low-pressure goal (e.g., "Read just 2 pages today") to get you moving again.
* **Quiet hours & Book Calling:** Optional notifications that gently nudge you when a book has been sitting untouched for too long, whispering that it's ready for you.

### 🔒 Private & Local-First
* **No Accounts Required:** Your library belongs to you. No sign-ups, no passwords, and no trackers.
* **Edge Computing Architecture:** All computer vision spine reconstruction, page OCR, and semantic processing run 100% locally on-device. Your data never leaves your phone.
* **Cloud Backup:** Your data is backed up automatically to your personal Google Drive (Android) or iCloud (iOS) through secure, private sandboxes.
